In the US survey this card was built on, a score of 7+ found 90% of people with hidden diabetes while referring 52% of adults. Among people scoring 7+, about 6 in 100 had hidden diabetes and 48 in 100 had raised sugar. Below 7: under 1 in 100.

Screening tool, not a diagnosis. Built from NHANES 2017–2020 (US); not yet validated in India. Anyone with thirst, frequent urination, weight loss or tiredness should be tested whatever their score.
यह स्क्रीनिंग है, निदान नहीं। ज़्यादा प्यास, बार-बार पेशाब, वज़न घटना या थकान हो तो स्कोर कुछ भी हो, जाँच करवाएँ।
